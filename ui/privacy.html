Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy describes how the 21Days mobile app and the Explore 21 Days website (together, the “Service”) handle information when you use Sahaja Yoga meditation course resources online.

Who we are

The Service is the Explore 21 Days online Sahaja Yoga meditation course companion (website and mobile app). For questions about this policy, email sahajabayarea@gmail.com or visit our Support page.

What the Service does

  • Search and browse meditation videos and handouts
  • Show upcoming and recent live meditation sessions
  • Browse session recordings
  • Play videos (including YouTube embeds) and open handouts
  • Optional local reminders before live sessions (mobile app only)

Information we collect

Information you provide

Search queries. When you search for videos or handouts, your query text is sent to our servers to return relevant results. We use this to provide search functionality, not to build advertising profiles.

Accounts. The current mobile app version does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Information stored on your device

The mobile app may store small preferences locally on your phone, such as:

  • Whether you enabled a live-session reminder for a specific session
  • Your chosen appearance theme (if you change it)

This data stays on your device unless you uninstall the app or clear app data.

Notifications (mobile app)

If you turn on “Notify 5 minutes before the session starts” on the Upcoming tab, the app schedules a local notification on your device. We do not send push notifications from our servers for this feature. You can turn reminders off in the app or disable notifications for 21Days in iOS Settings.

Information we do not collect

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the app. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Depending on how you use the Service, content or links may involve:

  • YouTube — video playback (in-app player or external browser)
  • Zoom — live interactive meetings (opens the Zoom app or website)
  • Google Fonts — typography may be loaded from Google when you use the website or app
  • Google Cloud — hosts our API and website

Those providers have their own privacy policies. When you leave our Service or open external links, their terms apply.

Server logs

Like most web services, our servers may automatically record technical information (for example IP address, request time, and error logs) to operate and secure the Service. We retain logs only as long as needed for operations and troubleshooting.

Children

The Service is intended for people interested in Sahaja Yoga meditation education. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please email sahajabayarea@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Summary: 21Days helps you explore course meditations and live sessions. Search text goes to our API to find content. Reminders are scheduled on your device. No account is required in the current app version.
